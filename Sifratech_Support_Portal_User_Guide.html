Portal Overview & Key Features
What the Sifratech Support Portal does and how it serves you

The Sifratech Support Portal is a dedicated, enterprise-grade help desk platform that manages all support interactions between your organisation and the Sifratech team. Every request, question, bug report, or change requirement is logged as a structured ticket, ensuring nothing is missed, every commitment is tracked, and all communication is documented in one central location.

100%
Transparent — every action is logged with a full audit trail
24/7
Always accessible — raise tickets at any time from any browser
Live
Real-time dashboard — your ticket data updates instantly

What You Can Do in the Portal

  • Raise support tickets — log any issue, bug, data request, enhancement, or training need directly through the portal form.
  • Track progress in real time — see exactly who is working on your ticket, what status it is in, and how long it has been open.
  • Communicate with the support team — add comments directly on a ticket and attach screenshots, log files, or supporting documents.
  • Review your history — access all past and present tickets for your organisation in the Tickets section.
  • Monitor SLA compliance — the dashboard clearly shows whether tickets are being resolved within the agreed timeframes.
  • Receive automated email updates — get notified by email whenever a ticket is created, assigned, updated, or resolved without needing to log in.
  • Accept or reopen resolutions — when a ticket is marked Resolved, you may close it formally or reopen it if the issue persists.

Supported Modules

The portal is pre-configured to handle tickets across the following functional areas of your ERP system:

Financials HRMS SCM PPM Sourcing Inventory Payroll Other

Portal Access & Login Credentials
How to access the portal and sign in for the first time

Step 1 — Open the Portal

Open any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox are recommended) and navigate to the portal URL provided by your Sifratech account manager. No software installation is required.

ⓘ
Portal URL The portal URL will be shared with you directly by your Sifratech account manager via email. Please bookmark the address once you have received it.

Step 2 — Enter Your Credentials

On the login screen, enter your assigned username and password. These credentials are unique to you and should not be shared with other individuals.

Sample Login Credentials
Username your.name@yourcompany.com
Password Assigned by Sifratech Admin
Role Customer / Support / Admin
Organisation Your Company Name
Your credentials will be issued by the Sifratech system administrator. If you have not received them or need a password reset, please contact support@sifratech.com with your name and organisation.

Step 3 — Sign In

Click the Sign in button or press Enter. The system will validate your credentials and redirect you to the Dashboard. If your credentials are incorrect, an error message will appear on screen.

⚠
Security Notice The portal displays "Authorised Access Only" on the login screen. Access is restricted to accounts provisioned by Sifratech. If you believe you should have access but cannot log in, contact your Sifratech account manager.

Step 4 — Logging Out

Always sign out when you have finished using the portal, particularly on shared or public computers. Click the sign-out icon located in the top-right corner of the navigation bar to securely end your session.

Creating a New Ticket — Step by Step
How to log a support request from start to submission

To raise a new support ticket, navigate to the Tickets section using the top navigation bar, then click the New Ticket button in the upper-right corner. A form will appear on screen. Complete all required fields as described below.

  1. 1
    Navigate to Tickets
    Click Tickets in the top navigation bar. The ticket list for your organisation will be displayed.
  2. 2
    Click "New Ticket"
    Press the New Ticket button at the top right. The ticket creation form will open as an overlay panel.
  3. 3
    Complete the Form
    Fill in all required fields marked with a red asterisk (*). Optional fields help provide additional context and are encouraged where applicable.
  4. 4
    Review AI Suggestions (Optional)
    As you type the incident title, the system's AI assistant may suggest a priority level, module, and ticket type. You may apply these suggestions or continue with your own selections.
  5. 5
    Click "Create Ticket"
    Press the Create Ticket button. Your ticket is immediately logged in the system and you will receive an email confirmation.

Ticket Form Fields

Field Required Description & Guidance
Project * Yes The name of your ERP project or system. Pre-filled as Oracle EBS R12 by default. Adjust if your organisation runs a different project identifier.
Detected Date Auto Automatically set to today's date. This records when the issue was first identified.
Incident Title * Yes A concise, descriptive title for the issue. Keep it to one clear sentence. Example: Invoice approval workflow not sending notifications.
Incident Summary Optional A single-line description of the error or issue to supplement the title.
Incident Type * Yes Select the category that best describes your request. See the type definitions below.
Module * Yes The functional area of the system affected (e.g. Financials, HRMS, Payroll).
Priority Optional Your assessment of the urgency. Defaults to Medium. The Sifratech team may adjust this based on business impact.
Environment Optional The system environment where the issue was found — Development, Patching, Testing, or Production.
Detected By Auto Automatically populated with your name based on your login. This is not editable.
Mobile No. Optional Your mobile number in case the support team needs to reach you directly for urgent issues.
Extension No. Optional Your office phone extension number.
CC Mail Optional Additional email addresses (comma-separated) to copy on all notifications related to this ticket. Useful for keeping managers or colleagues informed.
Long Description * Yes A detailed description of the issue. Include: steps to reproduce the problem, error messages displayed, expected behaviour versus actual behaviour, and any screenshots or logs you have available (can be attached via comments after submission).

Incident Type Definitions

Select the type that most accurately describes your request. Choosing the correct type helps the Sifratech team route your ticket to the right specialist and respond faster.

Incident Type When to Use Example
Bug The system is not working as expected due to a software defect. Journal posting fails with an error message.
Data Fix Incorrect data exists in the system and needs to be corrected. An employee's salary figure was entered incorrectly and needs amendment.
Enhancement A request to improve or extend existing system functionality. Add a new field to the purchase order form.
New Requirement A completely new feature or workflow that does not currently exist. Build a monthly financial consolidation report.
Reports A request for a new report or modification to an existing report. Produce an aged payables report by vendor category.
New Setup Request A configuration or setup task in the system. Set up a new cost centre and chart of accounts entry.
Training Request A request for end-user training on a module or process. Training session required for new Payroll team members.
Data Extract A request to export or extract data from the system. Extract all active employee records to Excel.
Responsibility Assignment A request to grant, modify, or revoke system access or responsibilities. Assign the AP Invoice Entry responsibility to a new user.
✓
Writing a Good Long Description The more detail you provide, the faster the team can resolve your ticket. A good description answers: What were you trying to do? What exactly happened? What did you expect to happen? What error message appeared (if any)? Which users or records are affected?

Sample Ticket — Filled Example
A realistic example of a correctly completed ticket submission

The following example illustrates how a well-structured ticket should look when submitted through the portal. Use this as a reference for your own submissions.

TKT-2026-0042
Invoice approval workflow not triggering email notifications to approvers
Open High
Project Oracle EBS R12
Detected Date 28 June 2026
Incident Type Bug
Module Financials
Priority High
Environment Production
Raised By Ahmed Al Rashidi
Status Open — Awaiting assignment
Mobile No. +971 50 123 4567
CC Mail manager@yourcompany.com
Long Description
Since the morning of 28 June 2026, the AP invoice approval workflow in Oracle EBS R12 (Production) is not sending email notifications to designated approvers when invoices are submitted for approval.

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Log in to Oracle EBS as an AP Invoice entry user.
2. Enter a new AP invoice and submit it for approval.
3. The invoice status changes to "Pending Approval" correctly in the system.
4. However, the designated first-level approver does not receive the standard approval notification email.

Expected Behaviour: The approver should receive an automated email notification with the invoice details and an approval link.

Actual Behaviour: No email is received. The approver only discovers pending invoices by manually logging in and checking their worklist.

Business Impact: Invoice approvals are delayed, causing payment processing to stall. Approximately 15 invoices are currently pending unnoticed. This is impacting vendor relationships and cash flow management.

Affected Users: All AP users across Finance department. Approver: Mohammed Al Seer (Finance Manager).
ⓘ
After Submission Once you click "Create Ticket", you will immediately receive a confirmation email. The ticket ID is automatically generated and will appear in the subject line of all future correspondence about this issue.

All Ticket Statuses Explained

Status What It Means Who Can Change It
New The ticket has just been submitted and is pending initial review by the Sifratech team. System (automatic)
Open The ticket has been reviewed and is awaiting allocation to a support engineer. Manager / Admin
Assigned A support engineer has been allocated to investigate and resolve the ticket. Manager / Admin
In Progress The assigned engineer is actively working on the ticket. Investigation and resolution activities are underway. Support Engineer
Awaiting Customer The support team has a question or requires additional information from you. Action on your side is needed to proceed. Support Engineer
Resolved The support team believes the issue has been fixed. The resolution notes are provided for your review. You must confirm or reopen. Support Engineer
Closed The issue has been confirmed as resolved and the ticket is formally closed. No further action is required. Customer / Manager
Reopened The issue has recurred or the previous resolution was insufficient. The ticket re-enters the active queue for further investigation. Customer / Manager
⚠
Awaiting Customer — Action Required When a ticket reaches "Awaiting Customer" status, your input is required for work to continue. Please log in to the portal and add your response as a comment on the ticket at the earliest opportunity. Delays in responding may impact the SLA resolution timeline.

Your Actions at Resolved Status

When a ticket is marked Resolved, you have two options available to you within the ticket detail view:

  • Close the Ticket — Select this if the issue has been completely resolved and you are satisfied with the outcome. The ticket will be archived.
  • Reopen the Ticket — Select this if the problem persists or the resolution has not addressed the root cause. The ticket returns to the active queue and the support team is notified immediately.

Service Level Agreement (SLA) Commitments
Response and resolution timeframes for each priority level

Every ticket submitted through the portal is automatically assigned a Service Level Agreement (SLA) target based on its priority level. The SLA defines the maximum number of hours within which the Sifratech team commits to respond and provide a resolution. SLA timers begin from the moment a ticket is created.

Priority Level Definitions

Priority Definition Examples
Top A critical system failure that is completely preventing business operations. The system is down or core financial processing is halted. System inaccessible for all users; payroll unable to process; financial period close blocked.
High A significant defect that is severely impacting daily operations. A workaround may exist but is unsatisfactory for continued use. Invoice approval emails not being sent; reports producing incorrect figures; key workflow failing.
Medium An issue that affects functionality but does not prevent core business operations. A reasonable workaround is available. A non-critical field displaying incorrect data; minor UI display error; single user access issue.
Low A minor issue, cosmetic defect, or general query with no significant operational impact. Label alignment issue; request for general guidance; non-urgent enhancement query.
Project A new requirement, large enhancement, or project-level deliverable requiring detailed scoping and planning. Build a new consolidated reporting framework; implement a new workflow module.
⚠
SLA Breach Monitoring The portal dashboard prominently displays any tickets that have exceeded their SLA target. If your ticket has breached its SLA, the Sifratech management team is automatically alerted and will escalate the matter internally. You do not need to follow up separately for SLA-breached tickets.

Dashboard & Reporting
How to interpret the live dashboard and use the ticket list view

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in. It provides a real-time overview of all support activity for your organisation. All figures update live — there is no need to refresh the page.

Summary Cards (KPI Strip)

Across the top of the dashboard, you will find six summary cards:

Card What It Shows
Total The total number of tickets raised by your organisation across all statuses.
Open Tickets that have been logged but are not yet assigned to an engineer.
In Progress Tickets currently being actively worked on by the support team.
Resolved / Closed Tickets that have been resolved and formally closed.
SLA Breached Tickets that are still open but have exceeded their agreed SLA timeframe. Displayed in red when greater than zero — these require immediate attention.
Ageing >3 Days Open tickets that have been unresolved for more than 72 hours. Displayed in red when greater than zero.

Charts & Visual Reports

Below the summary cards, the dashboard displays several visual reports:

  • Tickets by Status — A donut chart showing the distribution of tickets across all current statuses.
  • Priority Breakdown — A donut chart showing how many tickets exist at each priority level.
  • By Module — A bar chart showing which ERP modules have the highest ticket volumes, helping identify areas of concern.
  • Tickets by Type — A horizontal bar chart showing the breakdown by request type (Bug, Enhancement, Data Fix, etc.).
  • SLA Compliance — A bar chart showing the percentage of tickets meeting their SLA target for each priority level.
  • Ageing Tickets Table — A detailed list of all open tickets older than 3 days, showing the ticket number, summary, assigned engineer, age in days, and current status.

Using the Ticket List View

Navigate to the Tickets section to view a full list of all tickets for your organisation. This view includes the following tools:

  • Search — Type any keyword or ticket number into the search bar to filter the list instantly.
  • Filter by Status — Use the dropdown to show only tickets matching a specific status (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed).
  • Click any row — Click on any ticket row to open the full ticket detail view, which includes the description, comments, resolution notes, status history, and audit log.
ⓘ
Audit Log Every ticket maintains a complete, timestamped audit log of all actions taken — who changed a status, when, and any notes added. This provides a fully transparent record of the support process. The audit log is visible at the bottom of the ticket detail view.

AI-Powered Features
Intelligent assistance built into the portal to enhance speed and accuracy

The Sifratech Support Portal incorporates an integrated AI assistant that operates in the background to improve the quality and speed of support. These features are designed to assist both the person raising the ticket and the support engineers working on it.

AI Features
Smart Triage on Ticket Creation
As you type the incident title, the AI assistant analyses the keywords in real time and suggests the most appropriate priority level, module, incident type, and even a recommended engineer. You may choose to apply these suggestions with a single click or ignore them and enter your own values. This helps ensure tickets are routed correctly from the very first moment.
Ticket Analysis and Sentiment Detection
When viewing a ticket's detail page, the AI automatically analyses the content of the ticket description and assesses the likely urgency and the sentiment expressed. It then recommends the next action for the support team, helping prioritise workload and identify tickets that may need faster attention.
AI Reply Suggestion for Resolutions
When the support team is ready to mark a ticket as Resolved, the AI can generate a professionally worded resolution reply addressed to the ticket raiser. The engineer can use this suggested text directly or modify it before saving. This ensures consistent, clear communication in every resolution.
✓
AI Suggestions Are Guidance Only All AI-generated suggestions can be reviewed and overridden at any point. The final values for priority, module, type, and all other fields are always confirmed by a human before submission. The AI is a productivity tool, not an automated decision-maker.

File Attachments

You can attach files to any ticket at any time using the comment section within the ticket detail view. Supported file types include images, PDFs, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, and ZIP archives. Files are securely stored in the cloud and are accessible only to authorised users within the portal.

To attach a file, open the ticket, locate the comments section, click the paperclip icon, select your file, then post the comment. The attachment link will appear embedded in the comment.

Frequently Asked Questions
Answers to common queries from portal users
I submitted a ticket but have not received a confirmation email. What should I do?
Check your spam or junk folder first — automated emails can occasionally be filtered. If the email is not there, log in to the portal and check whether the ticket appears in your Tickets list. If the ticket is visible in the portal, it has been successfully submitted. If you still have concerns, contact support@sifratech.com.
Can I update or edit a ticket after it has been submitted?
Ticket fields cannot be edited after submission to preserve the integrity of the audit trail. However, you can add additional information, corrections, or context at any time by posting a comment on the ticket. The support team reviews all comments and will update the ticket accordingly.
The priority I selected does not seem right. Can it be changed?
Yes. The Sifratech account manager or team manager may adjust the priority level of a ticket based on the assessed business impact. If you believe a ticket requires a higher priority, please add a comment on the ticket explaining the urgency and business impact, and the team will review it promptly.
What happens if I receive an "Awaiting Customer" status on my ticket?
This status means the support team requires additional information or action from you before they can continue. Log in to the portal, open the ticket, read the latest comment from the engineer, and respond with the requested information by posting your own comment. Once you respond, the team will resume work on the ticket.
I marked a ticket as closed but the issue has come back. What can I do?
If an issue recurs after closure, please create a new ticket and reference the original ticket number in the long description. This ensures the history is maintained while treating the recurrence as a new incident for tracking and SLA purposes.
Can I see tickets raised by my colleagues in the same organisation?
Yes. All tickets raised by users within your organisation are visible to all users from that same organisation. This enables your team to stay informed about the status of all active support requests without duplicating submissions.
How do I request access for a new user in my organisation?
New user accounts are provisioned by the Sifratech system administrator. Please send an email to support@sifratech.com with the new user's full name, email address, job title, and the level of access required (customer access is standard). The team will confirm once the account has been created.
My password has expired or I cannot remember it. How do I reset it?
Password resets are handled by the Sifratech administrator. Contact support@sifratech.com from your registered email address with your username and a request for a password reset. Your new credentials will be issued securely.
Can I use the portal on a mobile phone or tablet?
Yes. The portal is designed to be fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets using any modern browser. No separate mobile application is required.
